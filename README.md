One word for the whole internet. The funniest, most fitting word wins, judged two at a time against a brief that weighs being one word first, then funny, then true of the whole internet, then surprising.

## The word

> touchgrass

10 characters. One word, lowercase, the way the internet itself spells it. Why this word wins, against the judge's criteria, most important first:

1. **One word describing the whole internet** — a single word for the entire internet, not a phrase, not a sentence, not a word about one app or one site.
2. **Funny** — a joke with no setup needed. "Touch grass" is the internet's own punchline, the thing the internet tells you to do when you have been on it too long. The word laughs at the internet using the internet's own voice.
3. **True of the whole internet** — every corner of it, from comment sections to doomscrolling feeds to group chats at 3 AM, is the place you have to leave in order to touch grass. It is not about one app or one mood; it is about what the internet does to everyone who uses it.
4. **Surprising** — it describes the internet by its opposite. Nobody's first reach is a word about going outside; the obvious answers are doomscrolling, brainrot, chaos, endless. This one turns the question around and lands harder for it.
5. **Lands in one beat** — ten letters, instantly readable, spelled exactly the way millions of people already type it every day.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# entry: touchgrass (10 code points)
```

Writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (byte-exact, no trailing newline), after validating the competition gate in code: 1–30 Unicode code points, non-blank, a single lowercase word with no whitespace.
